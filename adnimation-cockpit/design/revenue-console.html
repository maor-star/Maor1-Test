DATA QUALITY

The source computes total_revenue in a settlement pass that runs a day or two behind the raw figures. Until it runs, that column is left equal to publisher_revenue — fees are not deducted yet. Reading it directly would have reported a ~170% overnight jump in net revenue that never happened, and the anomaly detector would have fired a spike alert on an artefact of a job schedule.

gross_revenue and source_fee are both populated from the start, so the cockpit derives net = gross − fee and gets a consistent series on every day, settled or not.

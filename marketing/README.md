# Adnimation Marketing & Brand — Operating Files

Working repository for content planning, drafts, approvals, and brand reference.
Everything here is internal. Nothing in this directory is published by existing;
publication is a separate, deliberate act governed by `approvals/queue.md`.

## The two-list rule

Every content item lands in exactly one of these before it is written, not after.

**Publishes independently:** educational posts, distribution of already-approved
articles, name-free team and event content, explanations of company activity areas
carrying no numbers and no partner names, comments, routine newsletters.

**Draft for approval:** new blog articles, every success story, every partnership
announcement, any named demand or supply partner, anything touching Asian markets,
category reviews naming competitors, contrarian positions, positioning changes,
product or organizational announcements.

## The hard rule

Any content mentioning **any** name requires manual approval from the user before
publishing. Every time. No exceptions, and no carry-over from a previous approval
of the same entity.

A name includes: company names, brand and third-party product names, domains,
personal names, event names, acronyms, logos, screenshots where a name appears, and
indirect descriptions that identify unambiguously.

An item that would otherwise publish independently becomes an approval item the
moment a name enters it.

Beyond the user's approval:
- Publishers and clients need their own written approval, verified against the
  contract — not against the contact. Many publisher contracts prohibit name or
  logo use.
- Demand and technology partners need mutual sign-off on exact wording plus
  confirmation the integration is live.
- Text approval is not logo approval.
- Silence is not approval. No reply within a week means no publication.
- "We work with X" and "X chose us" are legally different statements. Never imply
  exclusivity, scale, or depth beyond what is contracted.

## Cadence

- **Sunday** — market review, build next week's calendar, submit approval items
- **Daily** — publish per calendar, comment round, check recent post performance
- **Monthly** — one-page performance report, next month's calendar, proposed blog topics
- **Quarterly** — message audit: what worked, whether the pillars still hold

## Channel volumes

- LinkedIn: 4–5 posts/week, Mon–Thu mornings ET, 80–200 words
- Blog: 2–3 posts/month, 1,200–2,000 words
- Newsletter: biweekly Tuesday
- Comments: 5–10/week on publisher and industry content
- Dedicated partnership announcements: **at most one per week**
- Category analysis: monthly post, quarterly deep-dive

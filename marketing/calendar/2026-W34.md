timeframe, and evidence of what held over time. I have none of those on file. Real
numbers only — inventing them is not an option, and blurring only works on a number that
exists.

This is the week's most valuable missing asset. A success story is the one format that
works for all three audiences at once, and it is also the only place the ~50% figure can
legitimately live. It is item Q1 and Q2 in the approval queue.

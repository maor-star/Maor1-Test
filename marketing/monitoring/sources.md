## How this file is used — and how it is not

**Extract topics and gaps. Never text.**

Adnimation's position on any topic is written from scratch, from our own experience
and data.

**Never:**
- Rewrite, spin, or produce "our version" of an existing post. Copied structure,
  hook, argument order, or examples are recognized even when every word differs. In a
  community this small, that costs exactly the category-leader standing we are
  building.
- Use another party's data or research as if it were ours.
- Copy a competitor's visual format, carousel, or template.
- Say anything negative about a named competitor.

**Do:**
- Take a shared topic and argue a different angle from our own experience and data.
- Respond openly to a named person's argument, quoting them properly, adding to it or
  disagreeing. **Requires manual approval — it contains a name.**
- Identify what everyone asserts and nobody proves. That is where the strongest
  content lives.
- Prefer commenting on the original post over publishing a parallel one.

**48-hour rule:** do not publish our own post on a topic raised by a monitored source
within 48 hours, unless the angle is materially different. It reads as reactive even
when it is not.

**Screenshots of others' posts are internal input only.** Never uploaded, never quoted
as an image, never used as a visual basis. Store them in `monitoring/screenshots/`
with a one-line note on the topic or gap they surface — not the text they contain.

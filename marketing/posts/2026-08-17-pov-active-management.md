## Copy

A yield tool nobody operates is a reporting tool.

Most monetization software is sold as access. You get a login, a set of controls, and a
documentation site. What happens next depends entirely on whether someone on your side
has the hours and the context to sit inside it every week.

Most publishers don't. Not because they're careless — because the person who owns ad
revenue at a mid-sized site also owns three other things. The controls stay at their
defaults. The defaults were written for an average property, and no property is average.

Software has no opinion about your inventory. It executes. Someone has to decide what
it executes, watch what it does, and change it when the market moves.

We took the other side of that. The technology does the work and our team runs it. That
is a harder business to operate and a slower one to scale. It is also the only version
where a publisher's revenue does not depend on their own free time.

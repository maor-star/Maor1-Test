## Copy

Cutting ad units to fix Core Web Vitals is a revenue decision, and it is usually made
without revenue data.

The framing is familiar. Performance on one side, monetization on the other, pick a
point on the line. It is a real trade-off. It is also measured over the wrong window.

A slow page loses the impression it was trying to sell. It also loses the session, the
return visit, and over a quarter the search position that brought the user in. Density
that looks profitable in a monthly report can be unprofitable in the annual one. The
cost shows up somewhere other than where the decision was made.

The opposite mistake is just as common. Stripping units to chase a score, with no
measurement of what the removed inventory was earning, is not optimization. It is a
guess with a green badge attached.

What resolves this is per-placement data: what each unit earns, what it costs in load
time and layout shift, and how both change by traffic source. Most publishers have the
first number. Fewer have the second. Almost nobody joins them.

Both numbers already exist. They are just sitting in different systems.

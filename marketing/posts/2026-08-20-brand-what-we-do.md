## Copy

Adnimation operates an agentic yield layer. Worth defining, since the phrase is new and
"we use AI" has meant nothing since roughly 2024.

Three parts, plainly.

It learns the specific property. Not a category benchmark or a vertical average — this
site, its sections, its audience, and how people actually behave on it.

It builds data layers the standard stack does not produce. A default setup describes
inventory in the terms that default setup was designed to describe. There is more to
know about a placement than that, and knowing it changes what the placement is worth.

It runs agents that act on pricing continuously. Not a floor reviewed monthly. Not a
rule set written once and inherited by whoever comes next. Pricing that moves while the
market moves.

What we do not do is hand it over and call it a platform. Our team operates it. The
publisher's side of the work is approving the integration and reading the reporting.

That is the whole model. The technology does the work, and someone is accountable for
running it.

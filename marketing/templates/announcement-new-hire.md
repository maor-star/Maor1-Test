# Template — New Hire Announcement

**Always an approval item.** It contains a name.

## Structure — five lines, fixed

1. Name and role.
2. One line on where they came from.
3. One line on what they will do.
4. One line on who in the audience it matters to.
5. One line of welcome, or an approved quote.

## Fixed rules

- **Identical length and identical enthusiasm for every seniority level.** A director
  and a junior analyst get the same number of words and the same tone. The template
  does not scale with title.
- One photo. Same visual treatment for everyone. Template: `new-hire`.
- **Never name a competitor they left.** "Comes to us from ten years on the sell side"
  is fine. The company name is not.
- **Never mention compensation.**
- Personnel matters beyond the announcement itself are never published.

## Skeleton

```
[NAME] joins Adnimation as [ROLE].

[One line: background, described by function and years — not by former employer name.]

[One line: what they will own.]

[One line: which audience this affects and how.]

[Welcome line, or approved quote from NAME.]
```

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] User approval obtained for this specific post
- [ ] Name spelling and role title confirmed with the person
- [ ] Person has seen and approved the exact wording
- [ ] No former-employer name in the text
- [ ] No compensation reference
- [ ] Photo cleared with the person; `new-hire` template applied unmodified
- [ ] Word count matches previous new-hire posts

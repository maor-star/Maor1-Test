# Template — Partnership Announcement

**Always an approval item.** It contains a name.

## Selection first — most partnerships do not get a post

Partnership volume is high. **Selection is the job.** Cap: at most **one** dedicated
announcement post per week.

| Tier | Bar | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | A name the market recognizes, or a connection that materially changes what the company can offer | Its own post. **2–4 per month maximum** |
| **Tier 2** | Real, live, but not market-moving on its own | Monthly roundup: "What we connected this month" — 5–8 lines, one connection per line, each with what it enables |
| **Tier 3** | Everything else | **Not published.** Logged internally, used as raw material for professional content later |

**A big name does not automatically earn Tier 1.** A flagship publisher or major demand
platform is worth far more as a full success story after 60–90 days than as a one-line
launch announcement. When there is a choice, wait and tell what happened.

The Tier 2 roundup is the point, not a consolation prize: it turns volume itself into
evidence of momentum instead of noise.

## Structure

1. What the partnership enables, concretely.
2. Who in the audience it matters to.
3. Optional approved quote from the other side.

## Never

- Publish before **written sign-off from the partner on the exact wording**
- Disclose commercial terms, volumes, or exclusivity
- Imply broader scope than signed. "We work with X" and "X chose us" are legally
  different statements
- Imply exclusivity, scale, or depth beyond what is contracted

## Skeleton

```
[What is now possible that was not before — concrete, one or two sentences.]

[Who this matters to and why. Name the audience segment, not every segment.]

[Optional: approved quote, attributed, exact wording as signed off.]
```

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Tier decision made and recorded — Tier 1 justified, or routed to roundup
- [ ] Weekly cap not exceeded (max one dedicated announcement per week)
- [ ] Monthly Tier 1 cap not exceeded (max four)
- [ ] User approval obtained for this specific post
- [ ] **Written** mutual sign-off from partner on exact wording, on file
- [ ] **Integration confirmed actually live**
- [ ] Text approval and logo approval obtained separately
- [ ] No commercial terms, volumes, or exclusivity in the text
- [ ] Scope claim matches the signed contract
- [ ] `partnership` template applied unmodified
